# Research References

This document contains links to foundational research papers relevant to this project.
Previously stored as PDFs in `lit/`, now referenced by URL to reduce repository size.

## Foundational Transformer Papers

- **Attention Is All You Need** (Vaswani et al., 2017)
  - The original Transformer architecture paper
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762

## Large Language Models

- **Language Models are Few-Shot Learners** (GPT-3, Brown et al., 2020)
  - Demonstrates emergent few-shot learning capabilities
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165

- **Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback** (InstructGPT, Ouyang et al., 2022)
  - RLHF methodology for aligning language models
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155

## AI Safety & Alignment

- **Concrete Problems in AI Safety** (Amodei et al., 2016)
  - Foundational taxonomy of AI safety challenges
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06565

- **Red Teaming Language Models with Language Models** (Perez et al., 2022)
  - Automated red-teaming methodology
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03286

- **Discovering Language Model Behaviors with Model-Written Evaluations** (2022)
  - Model-written evaluation methodology
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09251

- **Measuring Progress on Scalable Oversight for Large Language Models** (2023)
  - Scalable oversight metrics and methodology
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.03540

## Textbooks

- **Deep Learning** (Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville, 2016)
  - Comprehensive deep learning textbook
  - https://www.deeplearningbook.org/
